# Experimental software — use entirely at your own risk

**All use of this firmware, website, tools, downloadable packages and instructions is entirely at your own risk. Do not proceed if you cannot accept those risks.**

Installing or using this project can leave your keyboard unusable, erase mappings or other settings, interrupt input, fail during an update, or behave incorrectly. Experimental Bluetooth and power handling may disconnect or drain the battery unexpectedly. Keep another keyboard available and do not depend on this project for critical input.

Only one physical Lofree Edge has been used for the reported hardware tests. Hardware revisions, operating systems, browsers and RF environments may behave differently. Automated tests and emulation do not establish electrical correctness, reliable RF behavior or universal hardware compatibility.

## Recovery is not guaranteed

Backups, retained stock images, trial rollback and manual return shortcuts are **not guarantees**. Loss of recovery access may require opening the keyboard and obtaining external programming hardware. Recovery can fail. The project does not distribute Lofree's installer, stock firmware or stock repair payloads.

Before flashing, export your mappings, read the exact release notes, verify the image checksum and supported hardware, use a reliable USB cable, and keep another input device available. Keep the cable connected and the tab open until the transfer completes. Do not interpret a checksum match as a guarantee that firmware will work on your keyboard.

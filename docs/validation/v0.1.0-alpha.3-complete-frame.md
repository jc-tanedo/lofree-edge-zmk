## Limits and remaining evidence

- Current draw is unmeasured; no battery-life or measured energy-saving claim.
- Fn/media cold wake remains the known [issue13](https://github.com/jc-tanedo/lofree-edge-zmk/issues/13), not resolved by ordinary-row checks.
- The first disconnected-deep-wake key is consumed.
- Fresh pairing on this exact normal image was not repeated; saved-bond reconnect
  passed. New identity/unified-install firmware needs its own exact-image tests.
- Keyboard-initiated host remote wake, other hosts/hardware and broader coverage
  remain untested. Compiled tests are synthetic and do not substitute for these.
- Complete-frame decoding eliminates the observed failure in the tested workload;
  precise streaming callback/compiler root cause is not established.

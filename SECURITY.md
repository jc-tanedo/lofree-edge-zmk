# Security and sensitive reports

Experimental software, entirely at your own risk; no security certification or response-time guarantee is offered. See [RISK.md](RISK.md).

Use [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/jc-tanedo/lofree-edge-zmk/security/advisories/new) for exploitable issues. If it is unavailable, open an issue requesting a private contact **without** publishing exploit details or secrets. Maintainers should enable private reporting before launch.

Do not attach flash backups, Bluetooth bonding keys, credentials, device serial numbers or raw host/device captures. Provide minimal sanitized reproduction steps and the affected version. Browser device permissions are user-controlled; the website does not require accounts or upload mappings.
